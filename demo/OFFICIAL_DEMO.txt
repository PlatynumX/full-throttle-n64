Full Throttle DOS English demo
ScummVM target: ft

Upstream archive:
https://downloads.scummvm.org/frs/demos/scumm/ft-dos-demo-en.zip

The archive is intentionally downloaded during CI rather than duplicated in
this source ZIP. The workflow extracts the complete payload to:

    artifacts/sdcard/fullthrottle/

and creates:

    artifacts/sdcard/fullthrottle/saves/

This keeps the repository small while still packaging the demo files into the
actual build artifact used for hardware testing.
